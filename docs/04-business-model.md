# 04 — Business Model (Small Retailers)

This fills the blank "B2B (small)" line in the Spendy pitch. B2C stays **free**,
consistent with the overall model.

## Options considered

| Option | How it works | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-------------|------|------|
| **1. Commission per card sold** | Small % fee on the value of cards sold (online, and/or at the till) | Aligned with retailer success; no upfront cost | Only earns on *sold* cards, not store credit; needs volume |
| **2. Monthly SaaS** | Flat monthly fee for dashboard access | Predictable revenue | Small cafes won't pre-commit to a subscription for an unproven tool — high friction to adopt |
| **3. Free + paid extras** | Issuance free; charge for promo pushes, insights, loyalty tier later | Zero adoption friction; lands the flywheel first | Revenue deferred; depends on upsell later |

*Fee benchmarks (assumptions to validate): white-label gift card platforms
commonly take **3–6%** on cards sold; Square processes gift card payments at
standard card rates with no separate platform fee. Treat these as directional,
not quoted.*

## Recommendation

**Free to join, no monthly fee, a small percentage fee only on cards sold
online, and store credit issuance always free.**

Rationale:

- **No monthly fee at MVP.** Small cafes won't subscribe to something unproven.
  Removing upfront cost is what gets the first 10 retailers onboarded — and
  onboarding retailers is how the consumer flywheel spins.
- **Store credit is always free.** It's the acquisition wedge and the goodwill
  moment; charging for it would kill both.
- **Charge only where money already moves** — online card sales settling through
  Spendy's payment integration. In-store cards paid on the retailer's own EFTPOS
  can stay free at MVP, since Spendy isn't touching that money anyway.
- **Deepen revenue later**, once retailers are hooked and the data is proven, via
  promo tools, customer insights, and the loyalty tier (see
  [02 — Features](02-features.md)).

## The two sides

- **B2C:** free. The consumer never pays; the app's value is consolidation,
  balances, and reminders.
- **B2B (small):** free to join + small % on online card sales; store credit free;
  paid growth features later.

The model deliberately optimises for **adoption and the flywheel first, revenue
second** — the strategic asset in the early stage is the consumer users each
issued card brings in.
